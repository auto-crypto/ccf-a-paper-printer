# CCF-A Paper Printer ($CCFAA)

Believe me, anyone can publish a CCF-A paper.

**Chain:** Solana · **Supply:** 1,000,000,000 · **Fair launch**

## About

CCF-A Paper Printer is a meme coin inspired by the mysterious prestige of academic publishing. If a paper can have a title, an abstract, and enough confidence, surely anyone can publish a CCF-A paper.

Built for internet researchers, citation collectors, and professional paper printers, $CCFAA turns academic ambition into a playful on-chain meme. No peer review committee required—just bring your thesis, your printer, and your Solana wallet.
